Privacy Policy for Expense Assistant

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Expense Assistant is a personal project designed to help users record and manage expenses through WhatsApp.

Information We Process

When you interact with Expense Assistant through WhatsApp, the application may process information you voluntarily send, including text messages, expense information, and receipt images.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide Expense Assistant functionality, such as processing receipts, organizing expenses, categorizing purchases, and answering questions about expense data.

Third-Party Services

Expense Assistant may use third-party services such as the WhatsApp Business Platform and cloud services to receive messages and process data. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

Information is retained only as necessary to provide and develop the Expense Assistant service.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their interactions with Expense Assistant by contacting the application owner.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or requests concerning your data, please contact the Expense Assistant application owner.
